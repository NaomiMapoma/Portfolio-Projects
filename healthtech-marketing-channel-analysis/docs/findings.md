# 📋 &nbsp; Findings & Recommendations

## Marketing Channel Performance Findings

<b>1. Why has performance plateaued?</b>

<b>Creative fatigue on Meta -</b> Meta accounts for 48% of the total spend and 38% of the total acquisitions driven. However, Meta's CAC across the 12-week period swings from R$236.22 to R$462.48 alongside acquisition volumes staying relatively flat WoW with no clear directional trend. The same two creative variants have been running across the full campaign period with no refreshes, indicating that the current addressable audience has been exhausted due to creative fatigue resulting in the lack of scaled performance overtime.

<b>Insufficient top-of-funnel investment -</b> Meta & Google are demand-capture channels which work effectively with a pool of warmer audiences. TikTok, Creators & Affiliates are demand-generation channels which create this pool; however, the total combined spend of these channels only account for 19% of the total spend in comparison to the demand-capture channels. This could suggest as to why overall WoW acquisitions are stagnant.

## Efficiency Levers

<b>1. Redistribute Programmatic budget</b>

Programmatic is the only channel with a ROAS below 1.0 at 0.70 with CAC consistently high above R$800 WoW which is proving inefficient performance. The recommendation would be to redistribute this budget to the demand generation channels: TikTok & Creators.

<b>2. Optimise Affiliates to improve approval rates</b>

Affiliates has driven the lowest overall approval rate of 63.5% in comparison to the other channels indicating a low-quality and efficiency problem. The recommendation would be to audit the affiliate partner quality and traffic sources to identify whether the low approval rate is being driven by specific partners or the channel itself.


## Scaling levers

<b>1. Implement creative refresh across Meta</b>

Meta accounts for 38% of the total volume of acquisitions with a ROAS of 2.40 which means the channel has the capability to scale further. The plateau on Meta is a result of creative fatigue, given that the same two creative variants have been running over the course of the 12-weeks; therefore, the recommendation would be to introduce a creative refresh to expand the addressable audience pool to increase overall scale.

<b>2. Increase top-of-funnel investment</b>

The demand-generation channels: TikTok, Creators & Affiliates account for 19% of the total spend. For the demand-capture channels (Meta & Google) to continue to scale effectively, they need to be able to consistently capture this demand or the audience pool will become exhausted. Increasing top-of-funnel investment will ensure Meta & Google aren't targeting a static audience whilst contributing to the growth of the demand-generation channels overall.

<b>3. Scale Google by expanding search terms</b>

Google is the most efficient acquisition channel with a ROAS of 4.71; however, the volume of acquisitions are shown to be relatively flat WoW suggesting a plateau in performance. The recommendation here would be to scale Google by expanding beyond just branded or direct search terms to adjacent search terms and conditions.

## Limitations

<b>View-through attribution on Programmatic -</b> The performance dataset reports on new buyer, last-click and coupon attributed acquisitions. Therefore, there isn't a view on whether Programmatic is contributing view-through attributed acquisitions across other channels such as Google. A holdout study would be able to determine whether this hypothesis is true.

<b>Brazilian market -</b> All cost figures are in BRL (Brazilian Real) currency; therefore any benchmarks and key findings would specific to the Brazilian market and may not be relevant across other markets and regions.
